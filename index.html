Stakeholder Mapping

Map your key stakeholders, then watch them land on the power–interest matrix.

Fill a row for each stakeholder. Work down the table — the guiding question under each column heading is there to prompt your thinking. As soon as you set a stakeholder’s Interest and Influence, they appear on the live matrix below, coloured by how strong your relationship with them is.

A note on the two rating columns: Interest is how much they care about and engage with your work; Influence is how much power they hold over your work and its outcomes. The matrix needs both, so they’re their own columns here.

Power–interest matrix

Dot colour = relationship

  • Strong — well established
  • Developing — some way to go
  • Needs work — weak or new
Manage closely (high influence, interested): fully engage; help deliver their outcomes.
Keep satisfied (high influence, less interested): do enough to keep them onside, not so much they’re bored.
Keep informed (low influence, interested): keep them in the loop; they can be useful on detail.
Monitor (low influence, less interested): light touch, don’t over-communicate.

Priority relationships to build

High-influence stakeholders whose relationship isn’t yet strong will appear here as you fill the table.

Dot position is set by Interest (across) and Influence (up); colour shows relationship strength. Labels use the name, or the role if no name is set. Position uses three bands (Low / Med / High) with a small offset so overlapping stakeholders stay readable.
